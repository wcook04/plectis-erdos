# Experiment index

These investigations contain runnable examples, recorded computations and
research notes. Read each folder's guide or program docstring for its inputs,
dependencies, outputs and limits. The current claims remain in
[Results and limits](../../docs/RESULTS.md) and the [papers](../../paper/README.md).

A finite computation establishes only its stated finite result. A saved output
is a record of its own inputs and version, not a substitute for rerunning the
program or checking a general proof. Historical round names identify the
investigation they belong to; they are not additional current claim registries.

[The research guide](../README.md) locates Lean examples, adapters and workbench
sessions. [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how to return a useful
example, failed approach or correction with its evidence and attribution.
