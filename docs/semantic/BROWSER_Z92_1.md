## linear_and_affine_coefficients_remain_summable_under_half_geometric_decay

PROVED ANALYTIC TOOLKIT: the sequence j·(1/2)^j is summable, and for every real constant C the affine sequence (C+j)·(1/2)^j is summable as the sum of a geometric constant term and the linear term.

Class: infrastructure. Interpretation: authored_statement. Prior-art assessment: known_classical.

Scope: The lemmas show decay of an affine error envelope only. They do not prove tail nonintegrality, produce a certified kill without that hypothesis, establish cone supply, or imply irrationality on their own.
