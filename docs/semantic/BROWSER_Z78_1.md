## linear_predecessor_capacity_fits_inside_one_step_dyadic_room_from_rank_six

For every natural number c with c≥6, the linear quantity c-2 is at most the dyadic quantity 2^(c-4).

Class: infrastructure. Interpretation: authored_statement. Prior-art assessment: routine_corollary_or_specialisation.

Scope: The theorem only discharges the numerical room hypothesis for one step of future lookahead. It does not prove that the next greedy rank is skipped, establish the precritical suffix inequality on its own, produce cofinally many exact rows, or prove that one half belongs to the Mersenne achievement set.
