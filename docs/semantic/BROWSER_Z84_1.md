## dyadic_affine_envelopes_are_summable_with_exact_total

PROVED ANALYTIC TOOLKIT: for every real a and natural n, a/2^n = a(1/2)^n. The linear geometric series with terms j(1/2)^j is summable. More generally, for every real C the affine-geometric series with terms (C+j)(1/2)^j is summable and its exact sum is 2C+2.

Class: infrastructure. Interpretation: authored_statement. Prior-art assessment: known_classical.

Scope: The packet proves only convergence and an exact elementary series evaluation. It does not estimate a totient tail by itself, assert a property of Euler's totient function, fire a certifiedKill, supply certificates for unbounded periods, exclude a rational denominator, or prove irrationality of the Erdős 249 series.
