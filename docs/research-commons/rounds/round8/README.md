## What composes, and what does not

The separated-cut theorem succeeds beyond the fixed integer-lattice argument
because it controls the heights required by the Subspace Theorem. The calibrated
moment model shows why a decaying remainder alone cannot replace that control.
The signed interpolation theorem shows that many retained arithmetic
observations still leave the generating value free, but its signed coefficient
changes do not satisfy the positive support hypotheses of the Lambert theorem.
Combining the returns therefore supplies no full-support irrationality theorem
at base 3/2 and no solution of the original totient or prime-series endpoint.
