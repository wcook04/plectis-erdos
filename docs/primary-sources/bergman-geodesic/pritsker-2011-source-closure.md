## Read state and exact locators

The PDF text was extracted and read across all 15 pages. The title/abstract,
statement pages, proof pages, and references pages were also visually checked.

- **Identity and scope:** printed PDF p. 1 gives the title, sole author,
  arXiv identifier, abstract, and the paper's Green-potential and
  Blaschke-product scope.
- **Green-potential representation:** printed pp. 1--2, Theorem 1.1, defines
  the generalized pseudohyperbolic farthest-point distance and represents its
  negative logarithm by a Green potential with representing mass at most one;
  the simply connected strictness and support clauses are part of the same
  theorem.
- **Sharp potential inequality:** printed pp. 2--3, Theorem 1.2 and
  Corollary 1.3, state the sharp Green-potential inequality and the explicit
  unit-disc example.
- **Finite Blaschke translation:** printed pp. 3--4, Theorem 2.1 and
  Corollary 2.2, identify normalized zero measures with Green potentials and
  state the sharp supremum-norm inequality for products of finite Blaschke
  products.
- **Extremal arrays:** printed pp. 4--5, Theorems 2.3--2.4, state the
  equilibrium-measure convergence result and its partial converse for regular
  compact sets.
- **Proof mechanisms:** printed pp. 5--14 contain the Riesz-representation,
  Fekete-point, Green-equilibrium, lower-envelope, and Blaschke-product proof
  arguments, including the sharpness argument for Theorem 2.1.
- **Source bibliography:** printed pp. 14--15 were read; the references
  distinguish the cited potential-theory and polynomial-product literature
  from the result stated in this artifact.
