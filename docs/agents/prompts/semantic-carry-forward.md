# Semantic Carry Forward

Use when the conversation already contains the material needed to continue.
This is the portable version of the prompt labelled “Semantic Carryforward”
in the demonstration.

```text
Reconsider the whole conversation and continue from its strongest current
understanding. Identify the underlying problem, the useful results already
established, the constraints that still matter, and the most valuable remaining
uncertainty. Respect later corrections and distinguish evidence from speculation.

Look for a better way to formulate or solve the problem. A useful continuation
might connect results, simplify an argument, test an assumption, develop a
consequence or choose a more promising direction. Select what serves this task;
do not manufacture novelty or another checklist merely to keep going.

Do that next piece of work using the evidence and tools actually available to
you. Preserve important qualifications and exact identifiers when they matter.
Report the useful advance, why it follows, and what remains open. If you cannot
advance without additional evidence, name the specific missing fact and the
smallest useful way to obtain it. Do not present a recap as new progress.
```
