\subsection{Consumers}

The BooleanM\"obius lane (repo root \texttt{Erdos257PeriodNoncollapse/}, mirrored at
\texttt{Erdos249257/} for the modules that have been made public) is where the reduction
of Erd\H{o}s \#257 to a single arithmetic socket actually lives. Sixteen of its modules
have never been published: \texttt{BooleanMobiusCofinalExactRows},
\texttt{BooleanMobiusCriticalCapacityCofinal}, \texttt{BooleanMobiusCriticalCapacityGeometric},
\texttt{BooleanMobiusExactRowCrossing}, \texttt{BooleanMobiusExactRowDichotomy},
\texttt{BooleanMobiusExactRowDoubling}, \texttt{BooleanMobiusExactRowRankTwo},
\texttt{BooleanMobiusExactRowSeed}, \texttt{BooleanMobiusExactTransition},
\texttt{BooleanMobiusGlobalRepair}, \texttt{BooleanMobiusGreedyReduction},
\texttt{BooleanMobiusLocalRepair}, \texttt{BooleanMobiusSkipRow},
\texttt{BooleanMobiusSkipRowCofinal}, \texttt{BooleanMobiusSkippedCoreCriticalCapacity},
\texttt{BooleanMobiusSkippedCoreExactRow}; plus three sibling modules on the half-membership
track, also never published: \texttt{HalfGapMass}, \texttt{HalfGreedyFatalGap},
\texttt{HalfUpperResetCriticalBand}. Every one of the nineteen is covered below. Consumers
in this subsection are theorems whose conclusion is a genuine payoff
($(1/2:\mathbb{R})\in\mathrm{mersenneAchievementSet}$, or an infinite counterexample support)
but whose hypothesis is an unsupplied predicate. Every such hypothesis is displayed, not
buried in prose: that predicate is the actual target.

\subsubsection*{The load-bearing definitions}

\begin{defn}[257bm:d1 --- \texttt{ExactLocalMersenneHalfRow}]
For $n:\N$:
\[
\mathrm{ExactLocalMersenneHalfRow}(n) \;:=\; \exists D:\mathrm{Finset}\,\N,\ (\forall d\in D,\ 2\le d\le n) \ \wedge\ \mathrm{localPrefixQuotient}(D,n) = 2^{n-1}-1 .
\]
A finite Boolean support $D$ bounded by $n$ exactly hits the integer target $2^{n-1}-1$ under
floor-division Mersenne quotients at scale $n$ — the natural-number carry accounting is
exact, not merely close.
\par\noindent\ev{Lean} \scale{n/a} \coord{mobius-mersenne} \lean{ExactLocalMersenneHalfRow}{BooleanMobiusCofinalExactRows.lean:31}
\end{defn}

\begin{defn}[257bm:d2 --- \texttt{localPrefixQuotient}, \texttt{localMersenneQuotient}, \texttt{localRepairInteger}]
\[
\mathrm{localMersenneQuotient}(M,d) := \left\lfloor \frac{2^M}{2^d-1} \right\rfloor,\qquad
\mathrm{localPrefixQuotient}(D,M) := \sum_{d\in D} \mathrm{localMersenneQuotient}(M,d),
\]
both natural-number floor division, and $\mathrm{localRepairInteger}(D,k) := $ the signed
one-step carry defect of $\mathrm{localPrefixQuotient}$ at rank $k$ (see 257bm:i8 below for the
transition identity it satisfies). These are the three primitive accounting objects every
other definition and theorem in this lane is built from.
\par\noindent\ev{Lean} \scale{n/a} \coord{mobius-mersenne} \lean{localPrefixQuotient}{BooleanMobiusLocalRepair.lean:78} \lean{localMersenneQuotient}{BooleanMobiusLocalRepair.lean:20} \lean{localRepairInteger}{BooleanMobiusLocalRepair.lean:123}
\end{defn}

\begin{defn}[257bm:d3 --- \texttt{localBinarySuffix}]
\[
\mathrm{localBinarySuffix}(D,k,M) \;:=\; 2^{M-k} - \mathrm{localPrefixQuotient}(D,M) - 1,
\]
the leftover carry after subtracting $D$'s integer quotient contributions from the binary
expansion of $2^{-k}$ truncated at place $M$. Every capacity test in the lane is a bound on
this one quantity.
\par\noindent\ev{Lean} \scale{n/a} \coord{binary-digit} \lean{localBinarySuffix}{BooleanMobiusLocalRepair.lean:102}
\end{defn}

\begin{defn}[257bm:d4 --- \texttt{CofinalExactLocalMersenneHalfRows}, THE MASTER PREMISE]
\[
\mathrm{CofinalExactLocalMersenneHalfRows} \;:=\; \forall N:\N,\ \exists n:\N,\ N\le n\ \wedge\ \mathrm{ExactLocalMersenneHalfRow}(n).
\]
\textbf{Flag prominently:} this requires \emph{no compatibility whatsoever} between the
witness supports $D_n$ at different endpoints $n$. It is strictly weaker than a coherent
global trajectory (257bm:c1 below) — mutually \emph{incompatible} witnesses at each endpoint
are entirely sufficient. This weakening is simultaneously the whole reason the lane is
tractable and the exact place an attacker must be careful: a proof that looks like it
supplies coherent rows is doing strictly more work than is needed, and a proof that
looks like it refutes coherent rows says nothing about this predicate.

\end{defn}
