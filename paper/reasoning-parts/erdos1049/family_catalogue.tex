\section{Result registry and publication scope}
\label{sec:erdos-1049-complete-family-map}
The project registry records editorial classifications and result-atom
coordinates. These are retained in the accompanying archival catalogue,
not used as mathematical hypotheses or as a substitute for the theorem
statements in this paper. In particular, registry promotion status and
comparator selection are distinct from mathematical strength and from
the scope of a Lean build. The principal arguments are indexed in the
reading map and the formal-source appendix above.
