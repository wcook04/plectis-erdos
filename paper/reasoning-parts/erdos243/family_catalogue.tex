\section{Result map and proof dependencies}\label{sec:erdos-243-complete-family-map}
The following map is for mathematical navigation, not a catalogue of every
registered declaration. A checked component does not confer that status on
an assembled ordinary theorem.

\paragraph{Bounded defect.}
Canonical rational tail and normalised vanishing
$\longrightarrow$ eventual absorption; cofinally bounded negative values
$\longrightarrow$ stable gcd; reduced tail plus bounded rise
$\longrightarrow$ CRT contradiction. The state endpoint and the identified
canonical R7 transfer have supplied main-repository build evidence.
The extra bounded-defect assumption is not deduced from the problem.

\paragraph{Finite negative mass.}
$C_{n+1}\le C_n(1+(-E_n)_+/C_n)$ and summability
$\longrightarrow$ bounded integer height
$\longrightarrow$ finitely many rises and eventual constancy.
This scalar result needs no normalised vanishing. Its scalar and exact-orbit
endpoints are among the cited main-repository declarations; the separately
linked R8 canonical formulation is outside that receipt.

\paragraph{Weighted records and protected epochs.}
LCM records give weighted first crossings; primitive prime-power persistence
gives protected epochs. The canonical odd-power supply and the assembled
energy equivalence are proved ordinarily in this revision. Separate-release
integer lemmas and main-repository conditional record declarations retain
their own, narrower evidence boundaries.

\paragraph{Cubic rate.}
The quantitative tail comparison and Gamma-ratio estimate
$\longrightarrow$ integer cubic extraction; transport congruences and
Chebotar\"ev
$\longrightarrow$ a square in the cubic field; trace classification
$\longrightarrow$ two scales, each obstructed modulo seven.
The displayed assembly is an ordinary proof. Finite checks and component
sources are supporting evidence, not a formalised cubic-rate endpoint.

\paragraph{Static barriers.}
Sparse primes disprove a scale-free small-rise extension. At the specified
double-exponential modulus scale, prefix density and CRT give the exact
whole-modulus maximal-gap coefficient. Both are ordinary auxiliary results,
without a canonical reciprocal-tail realisation.

\paragraph{What remains.}
The unrestricted problem would require an additional global estimate, such
as the stated mass or record budget, or a contradiction to the full
nonterminal profile. None of the static examples or local identities
supplies one. The more detailed source registry and inherited declaration
catalogue remain in the research package rather than being repeated here.
