# Code of conduct

This repository exists so people can criticise, reuse, correct, and extend
public mathematical work. Participation should make that work easier to inspect
and safer to continue.

## Expected conduct

- Address claims, proofs, code, evidence, attribution, and decisions directly.
- Give credit to the people and sources that supplied material work.
- State uncertainty and surviving mathematical limits plainly.
- Accept good-faith correction and allow other contributors to disagree.
- Protect private or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, sustained unwanted contact, sexualised conduct, personal
attacks, deliberate intimidation, or publishing another person's private
information are not accepted. Discrimination based on identity, background,
disability, experience, education, or another personal characteristic is also
not accepted. Criticising a mathematical assertion or rejecting a contribution
on evidence is not, by itself, a violation.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, repository-linked
discussions, and other spaces where somebody represents this project. The
maintainer may edit or remove comments, close contributions, limit interaction,
or block an account when needed to enforce it. Decisions should identify the
conduct at issue without exposing confidential reports.

Report a possible violation privately through
[GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/wcook04/plectis-lean-erdos249-257/security/advisories/new).
Say that the report concerns conduct rather than software security. Reports get
a best-effort response from a solo maintainer, normally within a week. Do not
put sensitive details in a public issue.
