# Erdős 251 fixed-lattice block-residue computational lab

## Admission question

The target is irrationality of the prime-gap dyadic series.  Computation here
does not audit more primes and cannot prove the cofinal quantifier.  It asks a
mechanism question: after the data-dependent affine cylinder was proved
circular, does the replacement distance from the fixed lattice `2^r Z`
contain independent information about the prime-gap word?

## Exact decomposition

For one fixed shift write `X_N=T_h(N)` and let `B_(N,r)` be the observed
difference-digit block.  The iterated recurrence gives

\[
X_{N+r}=2^rX_N-B_{N,r},
\qquad
B_{N,r}-2^rz=2^r(X_N-z)-X_{N+r}.
\]

If `X_N` is a nonintegral rational, then for every integer `z`,

\[
|X_N-z|\geq \frac1{\operatorname{den}(X_N)}.
\]

Consequently any terminal bound `|X_(N+r)| <= U_(N+r)` forces block escape as
soon as

\[
2^r>2\,U_{N+r}\operatorname{den}(X_N).
\]

No feature of the intervening prime gaps occurs in this implication.

Lean checks the local implication as
`exists_blockResidue_escape_of_not_integral`.  More decisively,
`cofinal_blockResidue_escape_iff_not_eventuallyIntegral` proves that for every
`DyadicScaleDominates` bound, cofinal fixed-lattice escape is
equivalent to the non-eventual-integrality conclusion it was introduced to
prove.  Polynomial bounds are dyadically dominated, so the proposed use of a
polynomial prime-tail bound does not repair the mechanism.

## Discriminating computation

`check_erdos251_block_residue_no_go.py` constructs every reduced rational
initial state with odd denominator through the configured maximum.  At each
step it chooses the nearest integer digit, keeping the exact terminal state in
`[-1/2,1/2)`.  At the least depth with `2^r` larger than the denominator it
checks, using integers and `Fraction` only,

- the exact recurrence/block identity;
- the reverse-triangle lower bound;
- strict distance of the block from `2^r Z` beyond the terminal bound.

This grid is a regression for the proof coordinates, not evidence by volume.
Once the universal Lean equivalence is checked, increasing the denominator
range is explicitly forbidden as a progress claim.

## One-way consequence

Both affine block routes are now retired:

1. the data-dependent affine cylinder is exactly the original integrality
   predicate at its basepoint;
2. the non-adaptive fixed lattice, under every dyadically dominated bound, is
   exactly recoverable from rational basepoint nonintegrality and is cofinally
   equivalent to the desired conclusion.

The next producer must inject information not generated by the same dyadic
orbit—such as a genuinely external theorem controlling actual prime-tail
fractional parts or a different invariant that prevents the homogeneous
boundary mode.  Another block pullback, depth schedule, or larger prime audit
does not qualify.
