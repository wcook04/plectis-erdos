# Erdős #1041: an L2 selector for cyclic tetranomials

Status: analytic selector proved on 2026-08-24; the finite-family energy
identity, supplied-moment coefficient theorem, two-tail pigeonhole theorem,
end-to-end two-spoke composition, and resonant factor family are formalized in
`TetranomialL2Selector.lean`.  Its exact canonical shared-cache build passed

tetranomial class; it does not solve unrestricted Erdős #1041 and carries no
literature-priority claim.

## Headline theorem

Let

```text
g(w)=w^m+a w^r+b w^s+c,       m>r>s>=1,
```

have all its roots `w_1,...,w_m` in the open unit disk.  Put

```text
d=m-r,       e=m-s,
N=s sum_(alpha d+beta e=s)
      (-1)^(alpha+beta)/(alpha+beta)
      binom(alpha+beta,alpha) a^alpha b^beta.
```

If

```text
m(|b|^2+|c|^2)+2 Re(conj(c)b N)<m-1,                         (1)
```

then at least two distinct roots satisfy

```text
|c+b w_i^s|<1.                                              (2)
```

In particular, if the Newton-semigroup gap

```text
s != alpha d+beta e       for every alpha,beta in N,
```

holds, then `N=0`, and the Euclidean coefficient disk

```text
m(|b|^2+|c|^2)<m-1                                         (3)
```

is sufficient.

The Abel-tail theorem in `CyclicTetranomialCoefficientCase` makes the complete
origin spoke of each such root lie in `{|g|<1}`.  Their broken line through the
origin has length below two.  The conclusion also survives every translated
cyclic lift `f(z)=g((z-h)^q)`, `q>=2`, by the existing fibre mean-square and
spoke-pullback argument.

Condition (3) is a Euclidean disk, not the earlier diamond
`|b|+|c|<=1`.  It therefore proves genuinely new cases.  For example, in
degree five it permits `|b|=|c|=0.6`, since `5(0.36+0.36)=3.6<4`, while the
old coefficient sum is `1.2>1`.

## Proof

Write `p_k=sum_i w_i^k`.  Newton's recurrence up to `k=s<m` involves only the
two nonzero coefficient deficiencies `d=m-r` and `e=m-s`.  Inductively, a
power sum can be nonzero only when its index belongs to the additive semigroup
generated by `d,e`: if `k` is outside that semigroup, then the direct
coefficient at deficiency `k` vanishes, and every possible predecessor
`k-d` or `k-e` is outside the semigroup as well.  The semigroup-gap corollary
therefore gives

```text
sum_i w_i^s=0.                                             (4)
```

The easily checked condition `s<m-r` from the first version is a corollary,
because `r>s` implies `m-s>m-r>s`.  The semigroup form is strictly broader:
for `(m,r,s)=(10,7,4)`, the generators are `3,6`, so the fourth root moment
vanishes even though `s>=m-r`.

In fact the moment is coefficient-explicit in every exponent regime.  Since

```text
product_i (1-w_i t)=1+a t^d+b t^e+c t^m,
sum_(k>=1) p_k t^k/k=-log(1+a t^d+b t^e+c t^m),
```

and `s<m`, coefficient extraction gives the finite formula

```text
p_s = s sum_(alpha d+beta e=s)
          (-1)^(alpha+beta)/(alpha+beta)
          binom(alpha+beta,alpha) a^alpha b^beta.          (4a)
```

There is no contribution from `c`.  An empty sum gives the semigroup-gap
case (4); the first resonance gives `p_s=-s a`.  Substitution of (4a) into

```text
m(|b|^2+|c|^2)+2 Re(conj(c)b p_s)<m-1                     (4b)
```

is therefore an all-exponent coefficient-phase sufficient condition for two
safe tails.  The Lean companion checks (4b) for an arbitrary supplied exact
moment and composes the selector with the Abel theorem to produce two complete
safe spokes; (4a) is the finite formal-power-series coefficient calculation
written out here.

An exact integer enumeration confirms that this sharpening is substantial,
not cosmetic.  Through degree `80`, there are `82,159` triples `m>r>s>=1`.
The simple gap `s<m-r` covers `40,300`; the semigroup gap covers `68,299`,
including `27,999` additional exponent triples.  These counts only measure the
scope of the semigroup-gap corollary; the Newton recurrence remains proof
authority.

Put `T_i=c+b w_i^s`.  Expanding and summing squared moduli gives

```text
sum_i |T_i|^2
 = m|c|^2+|b|^2 sum_i |w_i|^(2s)
   +2 Re(conj(c)b p_s)                                     (5a)
 = m|c|^2+|b|^2 sum_i |w_i|^(2s).                         (5)
```

All roots lie in the open unit disk, so the last root sum is below `m`.
The weak estimate `sum_i |w_i|^(2s)<=m` already combines with (3) to give

```text
sum_i |T_i|^2 < m-1.                                     (6)
```

If fewer than two tails had modulus below one, at least `m-1` of their squared
moduli would be at least one, contradicting (6).  Thus two distinct safe tails
exist, and the Abel-spoke theorem closes the path.

The Lean theorem uses a finite index set and takes (4) as its moment input.  It
kernel-checks the full identity (5a), its nonzero-moment selector, (5), the
sharp `m-1` pigeonhole threshold, and the coefficient corollary.  The
specialization of (4) is the standard triangular Newton recurrence above and
is recorded analytically here rather than hidden behind an axiom.

## The first resonant layer

Identity (5a) also gives a useful theorem when the moment does not vanish.  In
the first resonance `s=m-r`, the inequality `r>s` implies `m-s>s`, and
Newton's identity is simply

```text
p_s=-s a.
```

Consequently the coefficient-phase condition

```text
m(|b|^2+|c|^2)-2s Re(conj(c)b a) < m-1                  (7)
```

is sufficient for two safe tails.  This enters the semigroup-resonant regime:
favourable phase correlation can pay for coefficients outside both the L1
diamond and the zero-moment Euclidean disk.  More complicated resonances are
handled by inserting the corresponding Newton recurrence for `p_s` into
(5a).

The signed region is nonempty beyond both earlier coefficient tests.  For any
`r>s>=2`, the exactly factored first-resonance family

```text
g(w)=(w^s-U)(w^r+q)
    =w^(r+s)-U w^r+q w^s-Uq                              (8)
```

has all roots in the open unit disk when `|U|,|q|<1`.  Its `s` roots satisfying
`w^s=U` have tail `-Uq+q w^s=0`, hence give at least two completely safe
spokes.  The Lean companion checks both (8) and the full spoke inequality for
every such left-factor root.

The rational quintic

```text
g(w)=(w^2-1/2)(w^3+4/5)
    =w^5-(1/2)w^3+(4/5)w^2-2/5                         (9)
```

has `|b|+|c|=6/5>1` and
`5(|b|^2+|c|^2)=4`, so neither the old L1 theorem nor the strict zero-moment
L2 disk applies.  Nevertheless its signed majorant in (7) is exactly
`84/25<4`.  Thus the resonant selector is a strict extension, not merely a
different expression of a previously solved region.

The deterministic root audit also sampled `3,204` admissible tetranomials.
Among the `912` exponent triples in its Newton-semigroup-gap lane, the proved
L2 coefficient disk certified `906`; all had at least two safe tails, and `7`
of the certified cases lay outside `|b|+|c|<=1`.  These counts establish
nonvacuity and strict computational coverage only; the identities and
pigeonhole proof above remain authority.

## Boundary and next step

The mixed moment is now explicit for every exponent triple.  The remaining
boundary is not a missing recurrence: it is the region where the signed
coefficient inequality (4b) fails.  That region requires a sharper use of the
actual radial energy `sum_i |w_i|^(2s)`, a different selector, or a
counterexample—not another reformulation of the same moment calculation.
