# What the failures teach, and which information would go beyond them

## The useful distinction

There are two mathematically different achievements. A structural theorem says what information a kernel contains. An arithmetic theorem controls a particular infinite weighted sum of that information. The short note gives exact structural results and a faithful actual arithmetic bridge. It should not pretend that a general matrix obstruction alone supplies the missing scalar statement.

## Rank and reduction

**Established:** successive threshold-column differences have disjoint supports. All finite sampled ranks are classified, and the ring normal form identifies their complete residue behaviour. For the `{2,3,5}` flattening, no denominator coprime to 30 causes a rank loss.

**Insufficient:** arbitrary-order rank, even accompanied by modular invertibility, does not force an irrational scalar pairing. The r5 rationalising rank-one perturbation remains an exact countermodel to that inference.

**What would add information:** an explicit arithmetic functional on the actual kernel and multiplicity data, together with nonvanishing, denominator control and analytic error control. Merely restating that a suitable functional would settle the target does not construct it.

## Uniform versus mean-square compression

**Established:** the normalised carry has a positive infinite uniform finite-rank approximation threshold. Its continuum operator also has singular values asymptotic to `(1-c)/(pi*j)`, so optimal mean-square rank-d error decays as `(1-c)/(pi*sqrt(d))`. The actual sampled matrices converge to that fixed-rank approximation problem after sorting their two phase lists separately.

**Insufficient:** a generic claim that the carry resists or admits finite-dimensional approximation. Both are true in different norms. Even an exact analytic approximation rate says nothing about the arithmetic quality of its coefficients until their denominators are controlled.

**What would add information:** a specified approximation family in the norm needed by the actual summation, with coefficient arithmetic and an error estimate of usable size. Growing-rank sample limits also need a quantitative discrepancy estimate; the proved fixed-rank limit is not a uniform theorem in d.

## Source statistics and signed redistribution

**Established:** in the actual pure-power height word, a bounded, positive-preserving coefficient redistribution can keep an arbitrary finite prefix, every coefficient modulo M, and both complete dyadic and ternary block totals, while prescribing any sum in an interval. The proof constructs capacity at actual five-power cells and uses a bounded vertex potential.

**Insufficient:** the claim that adding dyadic shell totals to generic bounded perturbation data automatically leaves only a thin set. That claim was proved in r5 only for a specific one-sided digit class. Signed local redistributions with positive final multiplicities have more freedom, as the new interval witnesses.

**What would add information:** an exact weighted constraint detecting the free five-channel potential; or a source-specific lift theorem that supplies the third family and its anchors. The existing all-three-channel rigidity theorem identifies the coefficients, but does not on its own prove anything new about their scalar sum.

A useful local question is to describe which additional weighted block identities annihilate the explicit potentials of memorandum Theorem 3.4. This is more precise than asking for “more source structure”: the countermodel provides an exact class the additional structure must detect.

## Carry lifts

**Established in the supplied corpus:** bounded lifts have exact weighted-defect identities. Complete unweighted channel-block nullity is a separate input to the channel-potential theorem. Prefix anchors can force the resulting potential constant when all channels are controlled.

**Insufficient:** bounded lift error, approximate orbit resemblance or unproved identification of weighted and unweighted defects. The new countermodel remains within a bounded distance of the actual dyadic tails and can make the modified tails eventually integral.

**What would add information:** a source-faithful construction giving the required unweighted block constraints, or a different contradiction using the weighted defect that is actually available. An eventual polynomial ansatz refuted at specific indices does not exclude every later-start or different-function-class ansatz.

## Residue witnesses and finite experiments

**Established:** the actual window criterion is equivalent to irrationality at a valid cap. The corrected all-multiplier shadow bounds and the modulus-five union-bound counterexample already landed in r5.

**Insufficient:** larger finite scans, a denominator-free distribution heuristic, or treating a sufficient random-subset condition as necessary. None of these supplies the infinite actual residue sequence.

**What would add information:** an actual residue construction or discrepancy estimate with its denominator range, start scale, window length and error retained. The finite Chinese-remainder construction remains a legitimate structured route even when whole-pool anti-concentration fails, but the requisite actual residues still need to be produced.

## Recommended division of research effort

The lowest-cost next formal theorem is the cut normal form over a localisation, starting from the already-authored difference engine. The most substantial new ordinary result to review is the two-channel interval theorem, especially the actual capacity and positivity steps. The spectrum is a separate analytic module explaining the norm trade-off.

For the parent problem, work on an exact weighted constraint or source-faithful lift that the explicit two-channel potentials cannot satisfy. This is not a claim that such a constraint has been constructed here. It is a falsifiable research requirement: test each proposed invariant on the supplied interval countermodel before treating it as evidence of irrationality.
