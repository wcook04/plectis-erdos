## A surviving “sparse branch” is empty

`a257_front.tex`, `ssec:route-5` (around line 1086), and `a257_invent.tex`, `sec:invent-R5` (around line 516), still propose a disjunction whose first branch is cofinally many super-logarithmic zero windows in the divisor incidence of a nonempty support.

For `a0=min A`, every `a0` consecutive integers contain a multiple of `a0`. Hence every such zero window has length at most `a0-1`, unconditionally. The branch is impossible. The proposed disjunction therefore reduces to its universal certificate branch; it does not split the remaining supports into two nonempty regimes.

Preserve the exact observation as `prop:257-no-growing-zero-windows`, then retire this “sparse/dense” division as an explanatory route. The separate lcm-gap criterion is not refuted by this observation; it concerns different information. This is a precise logical diagnosis, not a judgement that all sparsity methods fail.
