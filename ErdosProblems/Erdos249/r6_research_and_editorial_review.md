# Round-six research and editorial review

## 1. Verdict on the live note

The live note has reached the right scale. It already begins with the basis theorem, gives its evaluation-matrix proof, extracts the integral and periodic-coefficient consequences, proves the residue theorem, and finishes with one canonical arithmetic endpoint. Another wholesale reorganisation would undo useful work. The cover file's residue-series title is stale; the actual LaTeX and r5 disposition agree on the kernel lead.

The main mathematical promise survives the selective review. The composite-base retention condition is k∤r, the zero-channel argument needs two evaluations, and each matrix row makes only one primitive affine form prime. The proof has no simultaneous-prime assumption. The relation module is the module generated by the sections, not the saturation inside all integer-valued sequences. The finite-prefix variant requires late evaluations; the displayed argument supplies them. The full-depth and canonical formulations are equivalences, and their quantifiers remain indispensable.

The remaining editorial work is a transition problem. A reader should see why the successful bounded-residue argument does not simply extend to φ, then see which stronger information the actual-series criterion measures. The existing termwise-window obstruction supplies the missing transition. The existing one-prime rigidity theorem tells the reader what arithmetic the rational controls lose. Both are already in the attached corpus. Neither requires promoting an unregistered theorem.

The nine edits add only 11 whitespace-separated words overall and preserve the seven-page rendering in the tested build. All 20 current labels are retained. No new theorem is put on page one. The research candidates below are provided with complete ordinary proofs for separate review.

Tao's discussion of learning from a problem is useful here as a concrete criterion: the paper should leave the reader with an object, a mechanism, and an exact limitation, not a list of activity. His results-to-effort advice also supports extracting a cheap useful consequence rather than appending a large new section to every successful pass [T1–T3].

## 2. The strongest proposed structural result

Write the canonical kernel coordinates as X=φ(n), Y=φ(kn), and the retained positive-residue sections Z_(j,r). Let P be the prime divisors of k and put

    λ_T = k ∏_{p∈P\T}(1−1/p),   T⊆P.

The proposed complete relation ideal over Q(n) is

    ( ∏_{T⊆P}(Y−λ_T X) ).

“Over Q(n)” permits polynomial relations whose coefficients are rational functions of the index. Relations are required to hold eventually, so finitely many poles and the initial n=0 value cause no convention problem. The full unreduced truncation first undergoes its existing scalar reductions. The displayed polynomial is then the only remaining generator of the polynomial relation ideal.

At base two, every polynomial expression reduces uniquely using

    Y² = 3XY − 2X².

All normal monomials with Y exponent at most one are independent over Q(n). At base six, the roots are 2,3,4,6. The first nonlinear relation is quartic. This explains why a numerical check through degree three can see complete independence without contradicting the degree-four relation.

For q=k^e+1 canonical coordinates and h=2^ω(k), the rational rank of all products through total degree D, including 1, is

    binomial(q+D,q) − binomial(q+D−h,q),

with the second term zero below degree h. The Hilbert series is (1−t^h)/(1−t)^q. Increasing depth adjoins new polynomially free coordinates while leaving the finite branch degree h unchanged.

The mathematical content is completeness. The identity itself follows quickly from the local totient formula. The proof must exclude every additional polynomial relation involving the positive-residue channels. That is supplied by an elementary open-box argument, not by extrapolation from finite matrices.

The same presentation extends to n^a φ(n)^m, a≥0 and m≥1, with roots k^a λ_T^m. These are distinct positive numbers. This is a consequence of an injective substitution into algebraically independent totient sections. The case m=0 is excluded: pure powers have polynomial sections in n.

### The reusable mechanism

For pairwise nonproportional positive-slope affine forms L_i, the tuple φ(L_i(n))/L_i(n) enters every neighbourhood in a fixed nonempty open box on a set of positive lower density. This remains true on every prescribed progression.

First freeze all small-prime factors and the primes dividing slopes and cross determinants. Next assign disjoint sets of large prime divisors to the individual forms and tune their finite Euler products. Avoid the remaining intermediate primes. Finally average the uncontrolled large-prime loss. Its mean is bounded by

    s ∑_{p>Y} p^(-2) + O(log T / T).

The Chinese remainder theorem imposes the finite local constraints; a Markov bound controls the unprescribed factors on a positive fraction of the remaining progression. No affine value is required to be prime. The constants defining the box are fixed before the requested accuracy: otherwise the box could shrink and the argument would not establish algebraic independence.

A nonzero polynomial P(n,φ(L_1(n)),…,φ(L_s(n))) has a highest homogeneous part. Dividing by the corresponding power of n, and using the open box, gives a positive-density set where that part is bounded away from zero and lower-degree terms cannot cancel it. Thus the affine sections are algebraically independent over Q(n).

Now restrict n modulo rad(k). On each resulting class, Y=λ_T X, and X together with the Z variables remain algebraically independent. Every relation therefore contains the factor Y−λ_T X. The distinct factors are prime elements, so their product divides every relation. Monic division supplies the normal form.

The broader transport principle is useful independently of this benchmark: progressionwise algebraic independence plus finitely many periodic local multipliers turns a relation problem into a finite intersection of linear ideals. For an arbitrary finite affine totient family, group by primitive affine form, calculate its content multipliers on each residue class, and intersect the resulting kernels. The complete closure is a finite union of rational linear subspaces. Section 3 of the proof memorandum gives the exact maps.

### Antecedents and novelty boundary

This construction belongs to the classical Schinzel density tradition. Garcia–Udell–Yu explain earlier multidimensional density results and study additional prime-tuple restrictions under Dickson's conjecture [M1]. Halupczok–Ohst treat affine totient fractions and the finite range in the proportional case [M2]. Martin's theorem already implies affine linear independence [M3]. The exact all-polynomial kernel presentation was not located in the sources checked. That search outcome is not a priority claim, and the density mechanism should not be presented as if it appeared without antecedents.

## 3. A new theorem about actual residue-series values

The second candidate proves joint separation at distinct powers of a common integer base. Let b≥2, let D be a finite set of positive integers, and let f_d be rational-valued functions on Z/2^kZ. Then

    ∑_{d∈D} ∑_{n≥1} f_d(φ(n) mod 2^k) b^(-dn) ∈ Q

holds exactly when every f_d is constant on the even residues. The rational values have the explicit finite-exception formula in Theorem 4.1 of the memorandum.

For every fixed m≥3, this implies that 1 and all the values A_(m,b^d), d≥1, are rationally independent. For dyadic moduli it gives a two-parameter family: 1 and all A_(2^k,b^d), k≥2 and d≥1, are rationally independent. The corresponding higher-bit series form another independent family. Every relation is finite; the proof takes a common dyadic modulus and the largest bit index appearing in that relation.

This extends the existing one-base classification. It also differs from Wong's 2015 argument, where the denominator base equals the modulus [M4]. It concerns actual real numbers, while the polynomial kernel result concerns coefficient sequences. Those claims should be kept separate throughout the paper.

### Why the smallest dilation suffices

Clear denominators, remove rational constants and finite exceptional terms, and discard zero observables. Let d₀ be the smallest surviving exponent. Reindex the finite combination at base b:

    a_N = ∑_{d∈D, d|N} g_d(φ(N/d) mod m).

At N=d₀p, where p is a sufficiently large prime, a surviving d divides d₀p and is coprime to p. Thus d|d₀ and minimality gives d=d₀. Choose p in a totient residue where that observable is nonzero.

For each neighbouring offset t, choose a distinct auxiliary prime q_t≡1 mod m, larger than all dilations and the block width. Force q_t|d₀p+t. It survives division by every active d, and hence m divides the totient of every quotient appearing in that neighbour's coefficient. Every neighbour vanishes. The central coefficient survives.

The resulting mixed coefficient sequence is bounded and has arbitrarily long isolated nonzero pulses. The signed tail-lattice argument proves irrationality. Only one central prime progression is needed, and its reducedness is checked explicitly.

The domain restriction matters. The theorem treats bases b^d. It does not settle independence between arbitrary bases such as two and three. It proves linear independence of the values, not their algebraic independence. It says nothing about independence of the unreduced values S_(b^d).

### Finite witnesses rather than decimal inspection

The same pulse supplies a finite lower bound for any specified integer linear form. If |a_n|≤C, there is a pulse at N with half-width L, and η=C b^(−L)/(b−1), then under the explicit size conditions in the memorandum,

    |q₀ + ∑ a_n b^(−n)| ≥ b^(−N)(|a_N|−η)>0.

The certificate consists of primes, congruences and a tail inequality. The enormous prefix need not be computed because it becomes an integer after scaling. This is useful for proof checking. It is not a uniform irrationality measure: the constructed centre depends on the coefficients and may be very large.

The included exact checker constructs several such witnesses, including the difference A_(3,4)−2A_(3,16). It verifies primality, all quotient totients in each finite block, and the rational tail conditions.

## 4. What the stronger structural theorem teaches about failure

The open-box argument survives every o(n) perturbation. Its leading homogeneous term only sees normalised values. The existing rational control c differs from φ by at most two, agrees on odd arguments, stays between zero and n, and has binary sum 5/4. Consequently c(L_i(n)) has the same algebraic-independence property for every nonproportional affine family, even on prescribed progressions.

This is a stronger obstruction than a rank-only control. Arbitrarily large mixed-sectional ranks, and even the absence of all polynomial relations over Q(n) within such a family, are compatible with a rational weighted sum. The conclusion is about that information alone. The control need not satisfy the totient's exact scalar relations between proportional sections.

A second, separate control isolates the branch labels. Let t_j be successive products of odd primes. Change φ only at 2t_j, setting g(2t_j)=2φ(t_j). Then g−φ=o(n), g=φ on odd integers, 0≤g≤n, and

    (g(2n)−g(n))(g(2n)−2g(n))=0

for every n. At two adjacent positions on each altered dyadic chain the two multipliers are exchanged. No rationality claim is made for this new g. It demonstrates that the unlabelled algebraic branch equation forgets which residue class should select each multiplier.

By contrast, the exact labelled laws at one fixed prime plus an o(n) defect force g=φ. That rigidity is already proved in the supplied source. The two controls must therefore remain separate. Their hypotheses cannot be amalgamated into one imagined rational counterexample satisfying every desirable exact law.

This identifies information worth seeking in the parent problem: exact compatibility between a moving weighted prefix and the prime-divisibility classes selecting its local branches. Ordinary rank and large-scale normalised statistics discard that information.

## 5. The bounded-to-unbounded boundary

The joint residue theorem establishes genuinely new value separation within the bounded problem. It still fixes the coefficient bound before constructing an isolated block. The least-residue values satisfy

    0 < S−A_m ≤ (m+1)2^(−m),

but their individual irrationality and joint independence provide no lower bound against a rational candidate at that shrinking scale. The finite linear-form certificate depends on the auxiliary pulse centre, not just the original modulus. There is no uniform interchange of these parameters in the proof.

The already checked termwise-window result is an exact failed extension. Imposing 2^L|φ(N+L) forces the linear tail envelope above one. It cannot be compared with 1/q to exclude a denominator. Weighted cancellation remains possible and is exactly why the current canonical gap is the correct endpoint to keep visible.

For every c≥0 and positive odd v, the unsupplied condition is to find H>0 divisible by φ(v), with M=(2^H−1)/v, such that

    c+H+1 < (−B_(H,c)) mod M < M−(c+H+1).

The equivalence is already proved. Neither new candidate supplies the universally quantified H.

The other route boundaries should remain exact. Rank-uniform signed Hankel nonvanishing is already available in its stated range. The approximation route still requires reduced denominator times nonzero error to tend to zero. Direct Stern truncation has the wrong denominator/error comparison; changing the approximation family requires a fresh reduced-denominator calculation. The Euler-sieve approximation needs a family-specific separation bound that dominates its explicit limiting error. Prime-support escape must meet the actual carry residue, rather than being treated as an independent contradiction. Global binary normality does not force the prime-aligned phase gap, by the separate normal prime-locking control.

None of these observations retires an entire subject. Each names the particular information that is insufficient and the stronger comparison an argument would have to supply.

## 6. The adversarial verdicts

**Live basis: survives the reviewed attacks.** Composite bases, level one, finite-prefix deletion, the two zero channels, reduced prime progressions and the integral-saturation boundary have been checked against the displayed argument. No stronger formal statement is inferred from a module's name.

**Polynomial completeness: complete ordinary candidate.** Its vulnerable step is the fixed open box. The proof freezes the constants before accuracy is chosen and controls the unassigned factors in mean. Proportional forms are excluded from the density lemma and handled separately by finite local branches. The coefficient-field convention is eventual evaluation over Q(n). Both base-two quadratic and base-six quartic predictions agree with exact finite-field tests.

**Joint value separation: complete ordinary candidate.** The smallest exponent is taken only after zero observables are removed. The central prime exceeds every dilation. Auxiliary primes exceed both the offsets and dilations. Congruences are reduced before Dirichlet is invoked. Odd residues at a dyadic modulus produce only the two finite exceptions, which are removed rationally. Modulus two is a trivial rational boundary. Least residues require m≥3. Arbitrary independent bases remain outside the theorem.

**Rational-control strengthening: ordinary consequence with checked input.** Stability uses only an o(n) error; it does not transfer the full totient kernel relations. The new branch control has no asserted rational value. Both limitations are explicit.

**Lean candidates: uncompiled.** The candidates prove narrow interfaces with proof bodies. They do not package ordinary polynomial completeness or the full value theorem as a checked result. The environment had no complete runnable Lean workspace or installed lean/lake. The full build, Comparator and large continued-fraction computation were not rerun.

## 7. The publication decision and next mathematical question

For the live seven-page note, apply the nine precise edits and repair the public source coverage. Do not enlarge the abstract with every research candidate. The current lead, proof, residue result, amplifier and canonical endpoint form a coherent paper.

For independent mathematical review, check the finite-dilation pulse theorem first because it has a short proof and strengthens real-value statements. Then check the open-box lemma and the polynomial completeness theorem. Their conceptual payoff is larger: the kernel's entire polynomial algebra is a finite local branch structure with freely varying remaining coordinates.

The strongest follow-up for the structural programme is to classify polynomial section algebras of other multiplicative functions under an explicit progressionwise density hypothesis. Linear independence alone does not imply the required algebraic independence. The strongest follow-up for the actual-series programme is a quantitative separation argument that uses the labelled arithmetic branches and supplies the canonical gap. The present work makes that distinction more precise; it does not blur it by counting subsidiary theorems as a proof of S's irrationality.
