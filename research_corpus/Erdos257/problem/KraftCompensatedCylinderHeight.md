# Kraft compensation, exact carry deadlines, and the remaining parking law

Status: exact packet lemmas and a lossless residual transform, 2026-08-20.
The same-cylinder coalescence loss is fully absorbed by an ancestry/Kraft
coordinate.  The stronger Mersenne carry coordinate gives an exact deadline
for every power block and an exact \(8/15\) contraction after its first atom.
An exact protected-packet decomposition also separates carry credit from the
residual source, settling the local ownership problem.  These facts do not
yet give a Boolean expansion: the unproved interface is fixed-rank
stabilisation under the backward carries generated by later protected fronts,
together with lawful discharge of every surviving bank or escrow.

Write

\[
 w_r=\frac1{2^r-1},\qquad M(a,b)=w_aw_b.
\]

## 1. A continuous height which does not lose under coalescence

Attach a positive ancestry mass \(\lambda\) to a cylinder packet
\(kM(a,b)\), and put \(s=a+b\).  Its compensated height is

\[
 \Gamma(kM(a,b),\lambda)
   =s-\log_2\frac{k}{\lambda}.                       \tag{1}
\]

If packets on the same cylinder are merged, write
\(K=\sum_i k_i\) and \(\Lambda=\sum_i\lambda_i\).  Then

\[
 \frac K\Lambda
   =\sum_i\frac{\lambda_i}{\Lambda}
                 \frac{k_i}{\lambda_i}
   \leq \max_i\frac{k_i}{\lambda_i}.
\]

Consequently

\[
 \boxed{\Gamma(KM(a,b),\Lambda)
        \geq\min_i\Gamma(k_iM(a,b),\lambda_i).}      \tag{2}
\]

This removes the entire \(\lceil\log_2m\rceil\) loss from merging \(m\)
blocks.  It is not an estimate based on their real value: it retains the
amount of disjoint genealogy represented by each coefficient.

There is a compatible local Kraft allocation.  For an ordinary split of a
unit cylinder of stop \(n=a+b\) and ancestry mass \(\lambda\), give the
children of stops \(n+b\) and \(n+a\) masses at least
\(\lambda2^{-b}\) and \(\lambda2^{-a}\).  Their required sum is at most
\(\lambda/2\), so the unused mass can be distributed arbitrarily between
them.  Both children then have compensated height at least
\(n+\log_2\lambda\), the height of the parent.

For a collision between unit cylinders \(M(a,b)\) and \(M(c,d)\), where
\(a+b=c+d=n\), let \(\Lambda\) be the sum of their ancestry masses.  Choose
an endpoint \(e\), let

\[
 L=\operatorname{lcm}(e,n-1),\qquad
 A=\frac{2^L-1}{2^e-1}-\frac{2^L-1}{2^{n-1}-1},      \tag{3}
\]

and call the three unchosen endpoints \(u,v,z\), with multiplicity.  Give
the three ordinary children masses at least
\(\Lambda2^{-u},\Lambda2^{-v},\Lambda2^{-z}\), and give each of the \(A\)
copies of the lifted cylinder \(M(n,L)\) mass at least
\(\Lambda2^{-L}\).  The required fraction is

\[
 2^{-u}+2^{-v}+2^{-z}+\frac A{2^L}<1.                \tag{4}
\]

Here is a uniform proof.  Since \(A/2^L<1/(2^e-1)\), the left side is less
than \(1/7+3/4<1\) when \(e\geq3\).  If \(e=2\), its mate is \(n-2\) and
the other pair has sum \(n\).  The exceptional case \(n=4\) has all four
endpoints equal to two; directly, \(L=6\), \(A=12\), and the required
fraction is \(3/4+12/64=15/16\).  For \(n\geq5\), convexity of
\(2^{-x}\) on the integers gives

\[
 2^{-(n-2)}+2^{-u}+2^{-v}
 \leq \frac14+2\,2^{-(n-2)}\leq\frac12,
\]

so (4) is less than \(1/3+1/2\).  The unused ancestry mass again may be
distributed arbitrarily.  Every cylinder output then has compensated height
at least \(n+\log_2\Lambda\).  Since
\(\Lambda\geq2\min(\lambda_1,\lambda_2)\), this is at least one unit above
the weaker parent height.  The collision atom \(w_{n-1}\), labelled by
\(\Lambda\), preserves that weaker height exactly or improves it.

At a fixed atom rank the ancestry labels form an antichain.  Indeed, every
cylinder descendant has stop at least two larger than its parent stop, while
a collision emits only one rank to the left of its stop.  An event cannot
therefore have a descendant event at the same emitted rank.  If the ancestry
masses are realised as disjoint measurable pieces of a unit root label, the
total label mass of every fixed-rank atom cohort is at most one.  Combining
this fact with (2) shows that coalescence itself can never lower the physical
continuous height below the inherited compensated lower bound.

This is the clean answer to the digital-height counterexample: the lost bit
was exactly the bit of genealogy erased when two equal blocks were written as
one coefficient.  It does not yet say that the atom stream can be made
Boolean, because a Mersenne carry needs a specifically located cylinder
credit, not only a Kraft label.

## 2. Exact suffix credit and the deadline of a power packet

Define

\[
 \Theta_{r,c}
   =\sum_{q=1}^{c}2^{-q}M(r-q,r-q+1).                \tag{5}
\]

Repeated use of

\[
 \frac12w_{t-1}=w_t+\frac12M(t-1,t)
\]

gives the exact identity

\[
 \boxed{\frac1{2^c}w_{r-c}=w_r+\Theta_{r,c}.}        \tag{6}
\]

Consider a power packet

\[
 x=2^jM(a,b),\qquad 2\leq a\leq b,quad0\leq j\leq b,
\]

and put

\[
 c=b-j,\qquad r=a+b-j=a+c.                           \tag{7}
\]

The exact digital comparison yields, for \(0\leq q\leq c\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
 x>w_r+\Theta_{r,q}
 &\iff 2^{j+q}M(a,b)>w_{r-q}\\
 &\iff 2^a+2^b>2^{j+q}+1.
\end{aligned}                                       \tag{8}
\]

The last inequality holds through \(q=c\), because \(j+q\leq b\).  It
fails at \(q=c+1\), since then its right side is \(2^{b+1}+1\), while
\(2^a+2^b\leq2^{b+1}\).  Thus the exact capacity and deadline are

\[
 \boxed{\text{capacity }c=b-j,\qquad
        \text{deadline }r-c=a.}                     \tag{9}
\]

The word “capacity” has a literal carry meaning.  If two packets at rank
\(r\) have reserves exceeding \(\Theta_{r,c_1}\) and
\(\Theta_{r,c_2}\), then after replacing their two front atoms by
\(w_{r-1}\), the remaining reserve is larger than

\[
 \Theta_{r,c_1}+\Theta_{r,c_2}-M(r-1,r)
 =\frac12\bigl(\Theta_{r-1,c_1-1}
               +\Theta_{r-1,c_2-1}\bigr)
 \geq\Theta_{r-1,\min(c_1,c_2)-1}.                  \tag{10}
\]

The carried packet therefore has certified capacity
\(\min(c_1,c_2)-1\) and deadline

\[
 (r-1)-(\min(c_1,c_2)-1)
   =\max(r-c_1,r-c_2).                               \tag{11}
\]

The capacity in (10) is a guaranteed certificate; additional reserve can
make the true capacity larger.

## 3. The exact fixed-rank Hall law

Take \(N\) packets arriving at one rank \(r\), with deadlines
\(d_1,\ldots,d_N\).  Pairing equal-rank packets is binary carrying.  The
final occupied ranks are the positions of the one-bits of \(N\): a bit at
position \(s\) is one bin of size \(2^s\), parked at rank \(r-s\).

The bins have nested eligibility.  Hence the usual interval Hall condition
is both necessary and sufficient:

\[
 \boxed{
 \#\{i:d_i>t\}
 \leq N\bmod 2^{,r-t}
 \quad\text{for every }t<r.}                        \tag{12}
\]

The right side is precisely the total capacity of the one-bit bins whose
parking ranks are greater than \(t\).  Necessity is immediate.  For
sufficiency, order both packets and bins by decreasing deadline/rank; the
nested Hall inequalities greedily fill every bin.  Pair the \(2^s\) leaves
inside a filled bin to obtain its root.  All intermediate ranks are above the
root rank, so (10) certifies every carry.

For a homogeneous cohort of deadline \(d\) and capacity \(c=r-d\), (12)
reduces to

\[
 \boxed{N<2^{c+1}.}                                  \tag{13}
\]

This is the exact version of “the lift gain prepays the logarithmic merge
loss.”  The earlier bound by \(\lceil\log_2N\rceil\) recorded only the debt;
(13) records the available number of carry levels as well.

## 4. A lossless anchored residual transform

The suffix credit is not merely a scalar tail estimate.  After the first
atom of a power packet is removed, the positive residual can be put back into
one exact cylinder with the same deadline.  Let

\[
 D=2^jM(a,b)-w_r,qquad H=\operatorname{lcm}(b,r),
\]

and define

\[
 B=(2^H-1)
 \left(\frac{2^j}{2^b-1}-\frac{2^a-1}{2^r-1}\right). \tag{14}
\]

Both quotients implicit in (14) are integral after multiplication by
\(2^H-1\), and positivity is (8) at \(q=0\).  Therefore \(B\) is a positive
integer and

\[
 \boxed{D=B M(a,H).}                                 \tag{15}
\]

Binary-expand \(B\).  A bit \(2^hM(a,H)\) has the same deadline \(a\),
arrival rank \(a+H-h\), and anchored Kraft load \(2^{h-H}\).  The total
child load is exactly \(B/2^H\).

Put \(c=b-j\).  Direct calculation gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \frac{B}{2^H}
 &<D(2^a-1)\\
 &=2^{-c}R(a,b,j),\\
 R(a,b,j)
 &=\frac{(2^a+2^b-2^j-1)2^c}
         {(2^b-1)(2^{a+c}-1)}
 \leq\frac8{15}.                                    \tag{16}
\end{aligned}

The constant is sharp for the proxy \(R\), at \((a,b,j)=(2,2,0)\).  A
short proof is enough.  Write the last ratio as

\[
 \frac1{2^a-2^{-c}}
 \left(1+\frac{2^a-2^j}{2^b-1}\right).              \tag{17}
\]

For \(a\geq3\), it is at most \(2/(2^a-1)\leq2/7\).  For \(a=2\), split
into \(j\geq2\), \(j=1\), and \(j=0\).  The first two cases give at most
\(1/3\) and \(10/21\).  In the last case both factors decrease with \(b\),
so the maximum is the value \(8/15\) at \(b=2\).

In particular \(B/2^H<2^{-c}\).  Every bit of \(B\) therefore has
\(h<H-c\), and every child arrival satisfies

\[
 a+H-h>a+c=r.                                       \tag{18}

Equations (15)--(18) are a lossless renormalisation: the anchor/deadline is
preserved, arrival ranks move strictly right, and total anchored load
contracts by a universal factor smaller than \(8/15\).

For the root cylinder this begins with

\[
 M(2,3)-w_5=1510M(2,15).                            \tag{19}

The root load is \(2^{2-5}=1/8\), the first residual load is
\(1510/2^{15}=755/16384\), and the formal sum of the loads of all
independently expanded packet fronts is bounded by

\[
 \frac{1/8}{1-8/15}=\frac{15}{56}<1.                \tag{20}

Thus every single generation has ample homogeneous Hall capacity.  If a
frontier with common anchor \(a\) has total load less than one, then a cohort
of \(N\) packets at rank \(r\) satisfies
\(N2^{a-r}<1\), hence \(N<2^{r-a}\), which is stronger than (13).

## 5. Protected packets solve local reserve ownership

There is a more precise decomposition than (15).  For \(2\leq d\leq r\),
define the protected packet

\[
 P(r,d)=2^{d-r}w_d.
\]

By (6), it consists exactly of a front atom and its complete certified carry
reserve:

\[
 \boxed{P(r,d)=w_r+\Theta_{r,r-d}.}                 \tag{21}
\]

For the power packet in (7), a direct use of
\(2^bw_b=1+w_b\) gives the lossless ownership split

\[
\begin{aligned}
 2^jM(a,b)
 &=P(r,a)+2^{j-b}M(a,b)\\
 &=P(r,a)+2^{-c}M(a,b).                              \tag{22}
\end{aligned}
\]

The second summand is exactly \(2^{-b}\) times the original power packet.
Thus the atom and all credit needed to carry it to its deadline belong to
the protected packet; the new cylinder source is a disjoint algebraic
remainder.  The earlier \(8/15\) transform (15) bundled these two pieces
together.  Equation (22) gives a stronger \(1/4\) contraction for the
separately owned cylinder source, because \(b\geq2\).

Protected packets also have an exact heterogeneous carry law.  Suppose
\(d_1\leq d_2\leq r-1\).  Since
\(P(r-1,d_2)=2P(r,d_2)\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
 P(r,d_1)+P(r,d_2)
  &=P(r-1,d_2)+E(r;d_1,d_2),\\
 E(r;d_1,d_2)
  &=P(r,d_1)-P(r,d_2)\\
  &=2^{-r}(w_{d_1}-w_{d_2})\geq0.                  \tag{23}
\end{aligned}
\]

The carried packet has deadline \(d_2=\max(d_1,d_2)\), exactly as in (11).
If the deadlines agree, the excess vanishes and (23) is ordinary binary
carrying.  If they differ, the excess is strictly positive.  It has an exact
LCM description: with \(J=\operatorname{lcm}(d_1,d_2)\),

\[
 E(r;d_1,d_2)=2^{-r}
 \left(
  \frac{2^J-1}{2^{d_1}-1}
  -\frac{2^J-1}{2^{d_2}-1}
 \right)w_J.                                       \tag{24}
\]

Equations (22)--(24) prove the local reserve-disintegration predicate which
the scalar \(BM(a,H)\) representation did not expose.  No reserve is counted
twice: \(P(r,d)\) owns the front atom and its \(\Theta\)-chain, while the
contracted cylinder copy and every heterogeneous carry excess are separate
positive summands.

The heterogeneous excess also has a future-directed recoding which avoids
the huge coefficient in (24).  For \(p<q<r\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
 E(r;p,q)
 &=2^{-r}(2^q-2^p)M(p,q)\\
 &=\sum_{h=p}^{q-1}2^{h-r}M(p,q).                   \tag{25}
\end{aligned}
\]

The ownership split (22) is algebraic for every integer exponent
\(j\leq b\), including negative \(j\).  Applied to the summand with
\(j=h-r\), it produces a protected front at

\[
 R=p+q+r-h\geq p+r+1>r,                             \tag{26}
\]

and a cylinder remainder equal to \(2^{-q}\) times that summand.  Thus every
heterogeneous carry excess can be recoded entirely to the right of the carry
rank, with total cylinder remainder at most one quarter of the excess.

## 6. Escrow release and global rank stabilisation

It would still be incorrect to call (20)--(26) a Booleanisation theorem.
The protected-packet calculus makes every individual carry positive and
assigns its reserve without overlap.  Equations (25)--(26) also recode every
heterogeneous excess to strictly later front ranks with a one-quarter
cylinder contraction.  What is not yet controlled is the infinite sequence
of backward carries caused when those later fronts meet protected packets
already parked at earlier ranks.

There is an even simpler warning at the root.  Iterating only the ownership
split (22) gives

\[
 M(2,3)=\sum_{k\geq1}2^{-3k}w_2
       =\sum_{k\geq1}P(2+3k,2).                    \tag{27}
\]

These protected fronts are already distinct, so they are stable under (23).
They are not Boolean atoms: every one still owns the nonzero escrow
\(\Theta_{2+3k,3k}\).  Replacing each \(P(r,2)\) by its visible atom \(w_r\)
without separately normalizing those escrows loses positive value.  Thus
front stabilisation alone is not the desired conclusion.

A natural two-state linear potential cannot repair this omission.  Give a
power packet of capacity \(c\) the active anchored load

\[
 L_{\rm act}=2^{-c}.
\]

If a dormant escrow \(\Theta_{r,c}\) is released term by term, its active load
is exactly

\[
 L_{\rm esc}
 =\sum_{q=1}^c 2^{-(r+1)}
 =c 2^{-(r+1)}.                                     \tag{28}
\]

This escrow load behaves well under the later carry operation.  For
\(p<q<r\), the two input escrows in (23) exceed the output escrow by
\((q-p+2)2^{-(r+1)}\).  The active load of the future recoding (25) is
\(2^{-r}(1-2^{p-q})\).  Hence active plus dormant load changes by

\[
 2^{-(r+1)}
 \left(2(1-2^{p-q})-(q-p+2)\right)<0.               \tag{28a}
\]

Equal-deadline carrying preserves it.  Thus the obstruction below is not the
carry operation; it is the incompatibility of parking with later release.

Consider a proposed potential
\(L_{\rm free}+\alpha L_{\rm dormant}\).  Releasing an escrow changes its
contribution from \(\alpha L_{\rm esc}\) to \(L_{\rm esc}\), so
nonincrease requires \(\alpha\geq1\).  Parking
\(2^jM(a,b)\) by (22) changes its free load from \(2^{-c}\) to
\(2^{-(b+c)}\) and creates the dormant load in (28), where \(r=a+c\).
Nonincrease therefore requires

\[
 2^{-b}+\alpha c 2^{-(a+1)}\leq1.                  \tag{29}
\]

This conflicts with \(\alpha\geq1\) on a reachable state.  The ordinary
right-child path

\[
 M(2,3)\longrightarrow M(2,5)
 \longrightarrow M(2,7)\longrightarrow M(2,9)
\]

contains the power packet \((a,b,j)=(2,9,0)\), for which \(c=9\).  Equation
(29) forces

\[
 \alpha\leq\frac{8(1-2^{-9})}{9}
            =\frac{511}{576}<1.                    \tag{30}
\]

Hence no constant-weight potential of this natural active-plus-releasable-
escrow form is nonincreasing under both parking and release.  This failure
occurs before an LCM lift and is not a large-coefficient artefact.  A viable
potential must either be nonlinear/capacity-dependent or discharge escrow in
groups rather than term by term.

The ancestry-height bound (1) does not by itself prove finite return either.
If every front at rank \(r\) were assigned a *disjoint* label of mass at least
\(2^{5-r}\), then a fixed rank could indeed support only finitely many such
fronts.  But the ownership split (22) cannot preserve that lower bound on
both additive children.  At the root, \(P(5,2)\) requires label mass at least
one, while the residual \(2^{-3}M(2,3)\), whose next front is at rank eight,
requires another \(1/8\).  The required disjoint mass is

\[
 1+\frac18>1.                                       \tag{31}
\]

Assigning the same shadow label to both children avoids (31), but then a
descendant produced by releasing the parked packet's escrow can return with a
label overlapping the parked front.  Such a return need not merge a disjoint
branch, so the proposed ownership-union argument has no finite-return force.

The heterogeneous carry has the same sharp failure.  Two rank-\(r\) parents
at the lower label bound have total mass \(2^{6-r}\).  Their carried front at
rank \(r-1\) alone requires exactly \(2^{6-r}\), leaving no disjoint label for
the strictly positive excess in (23).  Therefore every ownership rule must
either let the excess lose the \(\Gamma\geq5\) bound or duplicate labels
between the main carried front and the excess.  Equations (22) and (23), not
ordinary coalescence, are the exact operations where the simple
Kraft-finiteness proof breaks.

## 7. Literal banks and the pooled power-packet law

The ownership failure does not make physical cylinder banks useless.  For
any two cylinders with \(M(a,b)>M(c,d)\), put

\[
 L=\operatorname{lcm}(a,c),\qquad
 K=\operatorname{lcm}(b,d).
\]

Then

\[
 M(a,b)-M(c,d)=A M(L,K),                            \tag{32}
\]

where

\[
 A=
 \frac{2^L-1}{2^a-1}\frac{2^K-1}{2^b-1}
 -\frac{2^L-1}{2^c-1}\frac{2^K-1}{2^d-1}           \tag{33}
\]

is a positive integer.  Numerical cylinder domination therefore upgrades to
exact positive LCM-cone domination.  Moreover,

\[
 \frac{2^L-1}{2^a-1}<\frac43 2^{L-a},\qquad
 \frac{2^K-1}{2^b-1}<\frac43 2^{K-b}.
\]

Since \(16/9<2\), the residue in (32) satisfies

\[
 A<2^{L+K-a-b+1},\qquad
 H(A M(L,K))\geq a+b.                               \tag{34}
\]

For a batch of \(2^j\) stop-\(n\) cylinders, the exact raw carry tax is

\[
 \mathcal T_{n,j}
 =\sum_{s=1}^j2^{j-s}M(n-s,n-s+1),\qquad
 2^jw_n+\mathcal T_{n,j}=w_{n-j}.                  \tag{35}
\]

There are \(2^j-1\) tax copies and \(2^j\) child banks.  If the banks are
assigned individually and each dominates its tax, (32) pays every tax with a
positive future residue and leaves one whole bank attached to \(w_{n-j}\).
That literal bank is genuinely stronger hereditary state than a minimal
\(\Theta\)-escrow.  It evades the \(9/8\) label overload for that finite
batch, even though it does not restore disjoint \(\Gamma\)-labels for every
residue.

Individual assignment is unnecessarily strong.  Let \(2\leq a\leq b\),
put \(n=a+b\), and take \(0\leq j\leq b\).  Pool the \(2^j\) copies of the
bank \(M(a,n)\).  Using \(2^aM(a,n)=w_n+M(a,n)\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
 2^jM(a,n)-\mathcal T_{n,j}
 &=2^{a+j}M(a,n)-w_{n-j}\\
 &=:D_{a,n,a+j}>0.                                  \tag{36}
\end{aligned}
\]

Positivity is exactly the power-packet law: the packet
\(2^{a+j}M(a,n)\) has first rank \(n-j\) and remaining capacity \(b-j\).
Splitting the original cylinder batch and paying the pooled tax gives the
exact hereditary rewrite

\[
 \boxed{
 2^jM(a,b)=w_{n-j}+D_{a,n,a+j}+2^jM(n,b).}          \tag{37}
\]

Both residual branches have fronts strictly later than \(n-j\).  Encode the
first branch by the anchored transform (14)--(18).  Relative to the parent
load \(2^{j-b}\), its load is less than \(8/15\); the second branch has load
\(2^{j-n}=2^{-a}2^{j-b}\).  Therefore

\[
 \frac{L_{\rm residual}}{L_{\rm parent}}
 <\frac8{15}+2^{-a}
 \leq\frac{47}{60}.                                 \tag{38}
\]

This pooled law bypasses the failure of individual bank depth on the first
generated cohort.  The \(340\) copies of \(M(10,11)\), of stop \(21\), have
individual natural-bank depth only six, so no assignment of single banks can
pay all eight levels of a \(256\)-batch.  Nevertheless (36) gives

\[
 2^8M(10,21)-\mathcal T_{21,8}
 =D_{10,21,18}>0,
\]

and hence

\[
 2^8M(10,11)=w_{13}+D_{10,21,18}+2^8M(21,11).       \tag{39}
\]

Thus the individual depth-six observation is not a counterexample to pooled
heredity.  The pooled identity is an exact, future-directed, contractive
normalization of every single power cylinder packet.

It still does not by itself settle collisions between atoms emitted by
different power packets.  Nor does it discharge an unspent bank attached to
an atom which never collides.  Those are precisely the cross-packet release
and stabilization requirements below.

## 8. Remaining global predicate

The remaining assertion can now be stated without scalar ambiguity:

> **Protected-packet stabilisation predicate.**  Starting from the protected
> packets and contracted cylinder copies in (22), repeatedly normalize equal
> front ranks by (23) and recode every excess by (25)--(26).  There is a fair
> schedule under which each fixed front rank changes only finitely many times.
> After a front stabilises, its escrow is lawfully released and normalized;
> the iterated release process is locally finite, all free cylinder and escrow
> mass tends to zero, and the limiting visible atoms are Boolean.

The \(2^{-b}\leq1/4\) contractions in (22) and (26), the nonnegative exact
carry excess in (23), and the Hall law (12) are the inputs for such a theorem.
They show that every *finite* collision cascade is funded and lossless.  They
do not rule out infinitely many arrivals from the right repeatedly changing
one fixed protected digit, and (27)--(30) show that stabilization does not by
itself discharge dormant credit.  This is a moving-boundary problem in exact
packet coordinates, rather than another coefficient-growth problem.  A proof
of the predicate would give a compactness route to Booleanisation; a reachable
genealogy with a nonstabilising fixed rank or nonvanishing released escrow
would disprove this normal form.

This isolates the global obstruction more sharply than either raw digital
height or scalar Kraft mass.  Coalescence debt and local reserve ownership do
not fail, and heterogeneous excesses themselves move right contractively.  The
remaining question is whether their returning binary carries and the later
escrow-release waves stabilise at every fixed rank.
