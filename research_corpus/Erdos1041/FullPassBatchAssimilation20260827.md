## Cross-return composition

The five substantive returns do not furnish five independent frontiers. They
collapse into three coordinates:

1. **Tree/attachment.** Return 006's `s_e(m-s_e)` observation is the unweighted
   shadow of the existing attachment-coupled selector. Return 008's curvature
   charge is a proposed analytic producer for the same missing joint transport.
   Their composition does not remove `(TR2)`'s common-ancestry factor.
2. **First-critical inverse branches.** Returns 007 and 010 agree that curved
   inverse branches are the canonical contained object. Return 010's straight
   substitute is already false, and return 007's new pointwise metric producer
   is now exactly false. This sharply narrows the surviving branch route to a
   non-pointwise/global selection mechanism.
3. **Near Fekete.** Return 009's qualitative blow-up motivation is compatible
   with the existing near-Fekete program, but it is weaker than the landed
   finite-cone/cusp results and does not prove its own half-plane transfer.

The superadditive delta is therefore the exact separation between two ideas
that the returns blurred: the `M,D` coordinate is valid and reusable, while
its pair-energy cone is not. Future work should not spend a packet trying to
prove `(PEC)` or the minimum-critical straight-spoke lemma.
