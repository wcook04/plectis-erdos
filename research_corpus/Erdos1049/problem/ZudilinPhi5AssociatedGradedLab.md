# Zudilin rank-eleven `Phi_5` local associated-graded lab

## Mechanism question

`Phi_5=p^4+p^3+p^2+p+1` is the first degree-four cyclotomic field in the
first-appearance sequence.  Its forced primitive residual-border order first
becomes positive at rank eleven.  The discriminating question is whether the
complete minimum determinant layer vanishes in `Q[mu,p]/(Phi_5)`, producing a
second factor invisible to the entry valuations.

This is a new residue field, not a longer `Phi_3` or `Phi_4` horizon.

## Local source construction

For source row `n`, write its known scalar content as

`h_n = Phi_d^e_d(n) u_(n,d)`,

where `u_(n,d)` is a unit modulo `Phi_d`.  A primitive entry then has leading
residue

`R_(i+j) u_(i+j,d) / (u_(i,d) u_(j,d))`,

with `R_n` the primitive source-row residue.  To recover `R_n`, it is enough to
construct the cleared source row modulo `Phi_d^(e_d(n)+1)`, divide its known
`Phi_d` power, and invert `u_(n,d)` modulo `Phi_d`.

The local constructor therefore reduces every multiplication immediately
modulo the required cyclotomic power.  Gaussian polynomials use their
division-free recurrence, while Pochhammer quotients are represented by exact
factor multiplicities.  This preserves source equality but makes the cost
depend on local precision rather than the degree of the global row.

Regression checks recover the previously committed exact residues:

- `Phi_3`, rank seven: `25863462 (p-1)` from 32 minimum assignments;
- `Phi_4`, rank nine: `-1655261568` from 80 minimum assignments.

The `Phi_4` local replay agrees with the global computation exactly while its
largest retained modulus has degree 24, independent of the much larger global
source-row degrees.  Source rows `0,...,4` are also compared directly modulo
their required local powers in every new run.

## Diagonal-Hankel reduction and parameter disappearance

Let `R_n(mu)` be the primitive row residue and put

`S_n(mu)=u_(n,d) R_n(mu)`.

The leading coefficient matrix is

`C_(i,j)=S_(i+j)/(u_(i,d)u_(j,d))`.

Thus it is diagonally equivalent to the Hankel matrix generated by `S_n`.
Every determinant term acquires the same nonzero factor
`prod_i u_(i,d)^(-2)`, so cancellation is governed entirely by the
minimum-support Hankel sum.  This is the analytic object a future all-index
proof must control; the local source computation is not an isolated numerical
determinant.

There is a second exact identity.  For every `d` and every `n>=d`,

`coefficient_mu(R_n)=0`.

To see this directly, write `P_m=prod_(1<=j<=m)(p^j-1)` and
`L_n=prod_(1<=r<=n)Phi_r`.  After dividing the source `A_n` coefficient by its
scalar content `P_n^3/L_n`, its `k`-th summand contains, up to a monomial and
sign,

`L_n/(P_k P_(n-k)) prod_(1<=j<=n)(p^(k+j)-1)`.

Write `n=qd+s` and `k=ad+r` with `0<=r,s<d`.  The exact `Phi_d` order of this
summand is

`1 + floor((n+k)/d) - 2 floor(k/d) - floor((n-k)/d)`

`= 1 + [r+s>=d] + [r>s]`,

which is at least one.  Thus every summand, not merely their sum, vanishes
modulo `Phi_d`.  This proves the row identity without extrapolating from the
computed cases.

At first appearance, let `A={0,...,d-1}` and let `P={d,...,2d}`.  For any
permutation, the number of `P->P` edges exceeds the number of `A->A` edges by
one.  A minimum-weight-one assignment therefore has no `A->A` edge and exactly
one `P->P` edge.  All of its moments satisfy `i+j>=d`, so the identity applies
to every factor in every minimum term.

Consequently the entire first associated-graded determinant is independent of
`mu` for every `d`.  Parameter tuning is eliminated from this mechanism
uniformly; the remaining analytic target is noncancellation of the resulting
parameter-free residue-class Hankel sum.  The `Phi_5` run records the row-level
vanishing range and checks the predicted determinant independence exactly.

## Assignment support

The all-index first-appearance theorem gives exactly `(d+1)2^d` minimum
assignments.  Thus the rank-eleven `Phi_5` residue is the signed sum of exactly
`6*32=192` classified assignments.  The checker treats disagreement with 192
as an invalid determinant graph, not as a numerical outcome.

## Exact result

The coefficientwise minimum-layer sum is

`5819278950 (p-1)(3p^2+5p+3)`

in `Q[p]/(p^4+p^3+p^2+p+1)`.  It is nonzero: its displayed representative
has degree three, below the degree-four cyclotomic modulus.  The minimum
permutation order is one, equal to the forced border order, and all 192
minimum assignments are included.

The row computation verifies `coefficient_mu(R_n)=0` for every required
moment `5<=n<=20`, and the final residues at `mu=0` and `mu=1` are identical
to the coefficientwise result.  Thus rank eleven has exactly one primitive
`Phi_5` factor for every parameter value; tuning `mu` cannot open a second
factor.

This eliminates first-appearance minimum-layer cancellation in the first
degree-four cyclotomic field.  Together with the all-index parameter-
disappearance proof, it leaves a sharper analytic target: prove
noncancellation of the parameter-free, diagonally Hankel minimum-support sum
uniformly in `d`, or find a genuinely different integral construction.  A
larger rank at `Phi_5` is not a licensed continuation by itself.

## Decision boundary

- A nonzero coefficientwise residue proves exact rank-eleven `Phi_5` order one
  and eliminates minimum-layer cancellation at first appearance.
- A zero residue opens a genuine second `Phi_5` factor and licenses extraction
  of the next local associated-graded layer.

The exact computation realizes the first branch.

Neither branch proves an all-rank content identity or irrationality at
`p=3/2`.  A larger `Phi_5` rank is not progress without an all-index mechanism.
