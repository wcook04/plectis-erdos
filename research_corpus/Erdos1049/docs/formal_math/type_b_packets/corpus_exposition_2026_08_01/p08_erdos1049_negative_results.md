# Assigned lens: Erdős #1049 negative-result exposition

Review `erdos-1049-rational-base-lambert.pdf` as a specialist in irrationality,
Padé methods, or arithmetic linear forms. The paper proves exclusions and
obstructions at rational bases; it does not settle the target irrationality
question.

The central decision is whether each negative result is stated as a precise,
reusable mathematical theorem rather than as evidence for or against the value's
irrationality. Audit the local-to-Archimedean obstruction, endpoint residues and
four-jet analysis, failure of coordinatewise clearing at `3/2`, cleared-tail
recurrence, height criterion at `7/2`, and homogenised Padé denominator
exponents.

Required emphasis:

- identify which exclusions are independently interesting to a specialist;
- state exactly which method or normalisation each theorem rules out;
- test terms such as jet, corridor, cone, clearing, and height against standard
  field usage and specialist misreadings;
- require primary-source research for any claim about the reach or limits of
  Padé and linear-form methods;
- decide whether the examples at `3/2` and `7/2` reveal a general theorem or
  should remain sharply local;
- inspect whether theorem statements are self-contained and whether the hard
  arithmetic step is visible;
- prevent a method obstruction from becoming an endpoint claim; and
- propose a replacement outline plus bounded title, abstract, opening,
  definition, hard-transition, and final-boundary specimens.

Prefer literal statements of what a method cannot do over internal language
about routes closing or being retired.
